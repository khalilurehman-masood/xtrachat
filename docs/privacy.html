XtraChat — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 September 2026

XtraChat is a Chrome extension that uploads an image or PDF you choose to a public file host and gives you back a link you can paste into a chat.

The short version

We run no servers, collect nothing, and use no analytics. The only data that leaves your device is the file you explicitly choose to upload, and it goes straight to the file host.

What leaves your device

Files you select. When you pick a file and press Upload, that file is sent over HTTPS to a free third-party file host, which returns a public link to it. Two hosts are used:

HostRetention
uguu.seDeleted automatically after about 3 hours
catbox.moePermanent

XtraChat measures how fast each host actually is on your connection and sends each upload to whichever has been faster, falling back to the other if one fails. Only the measured speed — a number — is stored, locally. The panel always shows which host was used and how long that link will live.

Three things follow from this, and you should assume all of them:
  • The uploaded file is public. Anyone with the link — or who guesses it — can open it. Links are not passwords.
  • Uploads cannot be deleted on demand. Neither we nor you can remove a file once uploaded. A uguu.se upload does expire by itself after about 3 hours; a catbox.moe upload stays up indefinitely.
  • Neither host is operated by us. Their handling of your file is governed by their own terms at uguu.se and catbox.moe.
XtraChat asks you to confirm you understand this before your first upload. Do not upload anything confidential, personal, or sensitive.

Scanned PDFs. If you upload a PDF with no text layer (a scan), XtraChat offers to convert its pages to images so chat tools can read them. That conversion happens entirely on your own computer using a bundled copy of PDF.js. The resulting images are then uploaded the same way as any other file.

What stays on your device

Stored locally through Chrome's storage.local API and never transmitted:

  • The floating button's position on screen
  • Measured upload speed for each host, so the faster one can be chosen next time
  • Whether the button is shown
  • Whether you have acknowledged the upload warning

Uninstalling the extension removes all of it.

What we never do

  • We do not collect, transmit, or store your browsing history, page contents, form data, credentials, or personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, trackers, advertising, or fingerprinting.
  • We do not sell or transfer your data to anyone. We never receive it.
  • We do not read or transmit the content of the pages the button appears on.

Children

XtraChat is not directed at children under 13.
