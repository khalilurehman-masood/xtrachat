How it works

  1. Click the floating button (drag it anywhere — it remembers where).
  2. Choose an image or PDF, or drop one onto the panel.
  3. Copy the link and paste it into your chat.

Supported files: PNG, JPG, JPEG and PDF, up to 25 MB. Scanned PDFs are converted to readable images on your own computer first.

One thing to know

Uploads go to catbox.moe, a free public file host. Anyone with the link can open your file, and anonymous uploads can't be deleted afterwards — so don't upload anything private. Details in the privacy policy.
